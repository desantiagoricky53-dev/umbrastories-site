Case File · True Crime

Hinterkaifeck

Bavaria, Germany · March–April 1922

Date
March 31, 1922
Location
Waidhofen, Bavaria
Status
Officially Unsolved
Victims
6 People

On the night of March 31, 1922, six people were murdered at Hinterkaifeck, a remote farmstead north of Munich in rural Bavaria: farmer Andreas Gruber and his wife Cäzilia, their widowed daughter Viktoria, Viktoria's two young children, and Maria Baumgartner, a maid who had arrived at the farm only hours earlier. All six were killed with a mattock, a pickaxe-like farm tool. In the days before the murders, Andreas had told neighbors about footprints in the snow leading from the forest to the farm — but none leading back — and about noises in the attic. In the days after, the farm appeared strangely alive: smoke rose from the chimney, the cattle were fed, and food had been eaten — signs that whoever killed the family stayed at Hinterkaifeck with the dead before vanishing. The bodies were found on April 4, 1922.

Hinterkaifeck remains officially unsolved. No person was ever charged. Suspects named were questioned but never convicted.
