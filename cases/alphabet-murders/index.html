The Alphabet Murders

Rochester, New York · 1971–1973

Date
1971–1973
Location
Rochester, New York
Status
Officially Unsolved
Victims
3 Girls

Between 1971 and 1973, three young girls from Rochester, New York — Carmen Colón, 10, Wanda Walkowicz, 11, and Michelle Maenza, 11 — were abducted and murdered. Each victim's first and last names began with the same letter, and each body was found in or near a town sharing that initial: Colón near Churchville, Walkowicz in Webster, Maenza near Macedon — the pattern that gave the case its two names, the Alphabet murders and the Double Initial murders. Carmen Colón disappeared on November 16, 1971, and was found two days later; Wanda Walkowicz was taken on April 2, 1973, and Michelle Maenza on November 26, 1973, each found within days. Over the decades police questioned several suspects, including a relative of the first victim and, much later, convicted California murderer Joseph Naso — but DNA testing excluded Naso, and no one was ever charged. The murders remain unsolved.

The Alphabet Murders remain officially unsolved. No person was ever charged. Suspects named were questioned but never convicted.
